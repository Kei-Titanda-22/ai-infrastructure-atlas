## 2. Question-to-claim contract

| Question | Public unit | Permitted P1 type | Scoring target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1. 何の会社か | 何をする会社か | 事実 | Company type and supplied function |
| Q2. AIインフラのどこにいるか | AIインフラでの位置 | Atlas分析 | AI role and value-chain layer |
| Q3. 主力製品・技術 | 主力製品・技術 | 事実 | Displayed product/technology portfolio |
| Q4. なぜ競争上重要か | なぜ競争上重要か | 会社見解 | The company's attributed positioning, not an Atlas fact |

Q1 and Q3 may not be expanded with P2/P3 detail. Q2 must remain an Atlas classification. Q4 must remain an attributed company view even when the underlying products are factual.

## 3. Global scoring rules

### Correct

- Contains every required element listed for that company/question, or an unambiguous semantic equivalent.
- Preserves the claim type. In Q4 this includes clear attribution such as「同社は〜と説明」「会社によれば」「自社の差別化要素として」.
- Does not add a material claim that is absent from the displayed P1.

### Partial

- Contains at least one required element but omits another required element; or
- gives the right Q4 mechanism without company-view attribution; or
- substitutes an adjacent P1 from another unit, such as answering Q4 only with Q2's Atlas role; or
- adds a material but non-dominant inference not shown in P1 while retaining the correct core.

Record the missing element or inference in `misinterpretation`; do not silently promote a partial answer.

### Incorrect

- Misses all required elements, contradicts a displayed P1, or identifies the wrong company/layer/product; or
- presents a materially broader invented claim as the main answer; or
- claims objective leadership, market share, monopoly, customer relationships, performance, future growth, ownership, or superiority that the P1 does not state.

### Semantic equivalents

The examples below are not a vocabulary test. An unlisted expression is acceptable only when it maps one-to-one to a required element without broadening scope or changing attribution. Brand names are not required when the key explicitly permits the product category. Generic language that loses the required layer, mechanism, or scope is partial.

### Unsupported inference

- Correct core + immaterial wording difference: score by the core.
- Correct core + material unsupported addition: `partial` and record `unsupported inference`.
- Unsupported addition dominates, contradicts, or changes scope: `incorrect`.
- Never use external knowledge to rescue an answer that is not supported by the displayed P1.

## 4. Q4 attribution rule

All five Q4 targets are `company-positioning` claims.

| Response pattern | Score | Required note |
|---|---|---|
| Correct mechanism and explicit company attribution | correct | `company view preserved` |
| Correct mechanism but stated as an independent fact | partial | `company view presented as fact` |
| Only the Q2 Atlas role, with no company-positioning mechanism | partial if adjacent relevance is clear; otherwise incorrect | `Atlas analysis substituted for company view` |
| Only a Q1/Q3 fact or product list | partial if it explains adjacent relevance; otherwise incorrect | `fact substituted for company view` |
| Objective leadership, monopoly, market-share, or superiority claim not displayed | incorrect | `unsupported competitive inference` |

## 5. NVIDIA

Public P1 IDs: `nvidia-overview`, `nvidia-ai-role`, `nvidia-value-chain`, `nvidia-products`, `nvidia-positioning`.

### Q1. 何の会社か

- Required for correct:
  - a data-center accelerated-computing or integrated computing-platform company; and
  - combines GPU/CPU-class compute with networking and software.
- Accepted semantic equivalents:
  - 「フルスタックのAI計算基盤企業」
  - 「GPUだけでなくnetworkとsoftwareも統合するdata-center platform企業」
  - “accelerated-computing infrastructure provider combining compute, networking, and software”
- Partial boundary:
  - 「GPU企業」「半導体設計企業」only;
  - identifies accelerated computing but omits the networking/software integration.
- Incorrect examples:
  - 「半導体foundry」「data center operator」「GPUを自社fabで製造する会社」.
- Inference handling:
  - market dominance, customer names, or in-house fabrication are not displayed and cannot support correct.

### Q2. AIインフラのどこにいるか

- Required for correct:
  - integrates AI-factory compute, networking, and base software; and
  - sits on the fabless side from advanced-chip design through AI-cluster system infrastructure, with manufacturing dependent on external foundries.
- Accepted semantic equivalents:
  - 「AI clusterの計算・接続・software基盤を束ねるfabless platform層」
  - 「chipを設計しcluster基盤まで担うが、製造は外部foundry」.
- Partial boundary:
  - states only the integrated AI-factory role; or
  - states only fabless chip design/external manufacturing.
- Incorrect examples:
  - 「wafer製造工程」「data centerの電力・冷却層」「cloud service運営層」.
- Inference handling:
  - a specific foundry, manufacturing share, or customer is outside P1.

### Q3. 主力製品・技術

- Required for correct:
  - GPU; and
  - at least two of CPU, DPU, or networking, showing portfolio breadth.
- Accepted semantic equivalents:
  - Blackwell/GPU, Grace/CPU, BlueField/DPU, Spectrum-X/network;
  - 「accelerator、processor、data-processing unit、AI network」.
- Partial boundary:
  - GPU only; or GPU plus only one other displayed category.
- Incorrect examples:
  - fabrication equipment, advanced packaging, UPS, or optical cable as NVIDIA's main portfolio.
- Inference handling:
  - benchmark performance, product generations not displayed, and sales mix are not scored.

### Q4. なぜ競争上重要か

- Required for correct:
  - attributes the view to NVIDIA; and
  - says components are jointly designed or integrated as one AI platform; and
  - covers compute plus networking/software breadth.
- Accepted semantic equivalents:
  - 「NVIDIAはGPU、network、softwareをco-designしたend-to-end AI platformと自社を位置付ける」
  - “the company says its importance comes from co-designing compute, networking, optics, and software as one platform.”
- Partial boundary:
  - correct integration mechanism without company attribution;
  - a broad product list without joint-design/integration;
  - only「AI-factoryの中核」from Atlas analysis.
- Incorrect examples:
  - 「市場shareが最大」「競合がいない」「全AI chipを自社製造するから」.
- Inference handling:
  - “most important,” “dominant,” switching costs, ecosystem lock-in, and performance leadership are not displayed P1 claims.
